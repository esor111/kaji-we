## 🚀 What You Need to Do (2 Steps)

### Step 1: Restart Kiro

**Command Palette:**
- Press `Ctrl+Shift+P` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+P` (Mac)
- Type "Reload Window"
- Press Enter

**Or:**
- Close and reopen Kiro

### Step 2: Authenticate

After restart:
1. Browser window opens automatically
2. Login to Supabase
3. Grant access to MCP server
4. Choose your organization
5. Done!
