## 🎨 How to Make This REALLY Great

### Design Excellence
1. **Visual Hierarchy**: Your Hero section is strong with Nepali text - maintain this cultural connection throughout
2. **Consistency**: Ensure spacing, colors, and typography are consistent across all sections
3. **White Space**: Don't overcrowd - let content breathe
4. **Mobile-First**: 70%+ traffic in Nepal is mobile - prioritize this

### Content Strategy
1. **Local SEO**: Target keywords like "waterproofing Kathmandu", "basement waterproofing Nepal"
2. **Social Proof**: Real photos of your work > stock photos
3. **Educational Content**: Blog about monsoon preparation, common water damage issues in Nepal
4. **Clear CTAs**: Make it dead simple to contact you

### User Experience
1. **Speed**: Optimize images - slow sites lose customers
2. **Trust Signals**: Display certifications, warranties, insurance prominently
3. **Easy Contact**: Multiple ways to reach you (phone, WhatsApp, form)
4. **Proof**: Before/after galleries, video testimonials

### Technical Excellence
1. **Performance**: Aim for 90+ Lighthouse score
2. **Accessibility**: Ensure keyboard navigation, screen reader support
3. **SEO**: Proper meta tags, structured data, sitemap
4. **Security**: HTTPS, secure forms, privacy policy
